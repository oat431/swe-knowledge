# Engineering Manager

> **Positioning:** A people and delivery leadership path focused on creating the conditions for engineers and teams to deliver useful, sustainable results.

## What This Path Is

An Engineering Manager is responsible for the team's ability to deliver consistently, develop its people, maintain healthy working relationships, and operate within the organization's wider context.

The role is not simply a senior engineer with more meetings. Its center of gravity is people leadership: career development, hiring, coaching, performance, team health, communication, and organizational alignment. Technical understanding remains important, but the manager creates leverage through people and systems.

## Primary Outcomes

- A healthy, capable, and appropriately staffed engineering team
- Clear goals, priorities, and delivery expectations
- Engineers who receive useful feedback and career support
- Sustainable execution and transparent risk communication
- Collaboration with product, design, operations, security, and management
- Continuous improvement in team process and organizational effectiveness

## Signals for Moving Forward

- You gain satisfaction from helping other people succeed.
- You can make team decisions that are fair, clear, and aligned with organizational needs.
- You can handle difficult conversations without avoiding them or making them personal.
- You are willing to measure your success through team outcomes rather than personal code output.
- You can preserve technical credibility while delegating implementation ownership.

## Evidence to Build

- Team charter and working agreement
- Hiring or onboarding plan
- Career development plan
- One-to-one structure
- Team health or capability assessment
- Delivery and risk review
- Performance feedback examples
- Process improvement proposal
- Stakeholder communication plan
