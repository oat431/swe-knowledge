# Tech Lead

> **Positioning:** A technical leadership role centered on the system, the technical direction, and the team's ability to deliver. It may be a formal position or a responsibility assigned to an experienced engineer.

## What This Path Is

The Tech Lead is responsible for creating a coherent technical result with a team. The role balances hands-on engineering, architecture, technical mentoring, coordination, and production support.

A useful distinction is: the Tech Lead is primarily accountable for the **system and technical outcome**, while the Engineering Manager is primarily accountable for the **people and team conditions**. In smaller teams, one person may perform both roles.

## Primary Outcomes

- A technical direction understood by the team
- Design and implementation choices that fit the system's needs
- Technical risks and dependencies actively managed
- Healthy production operation and monitoring
- Constructive technical discussion and decision making
- Engineers who can work independently with shared context


## Signals for Moving Forward

- The team looks to you for technical clarity during ambiguity.
- You can make progress without becoming the bottleneck for every decision.
- You can disagree constructively and help the team commit to a decision.
- You understand both development work and production consequences.
- You can represent technical concerns to product, delivery, and management partners.

## Evidence to Build

- Team technical roadmap
- Architecture Decision Records
- System design presentation
- Production-readiness checklist
- Incident response plan and post-incident review
- Technical spike or proof of concept
- Team development or mentoring plan
