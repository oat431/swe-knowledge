# Delivery and Client Management

> **Core capability:** The independent plans and executes work with explicit commitments — delivering reliably, communicating honestly, managing multiple clients, and closing engagements well.

## Why This Matters

Delivery is where independent reputation is built or burned. A solo practitioner has no team to absorb problems, no brand to hide behind, and no second chance at a first project. The discipline that compensates: explicit commitments (statements of work), honest communication rhythms, disciplined scope control, and a quality bar held without a reviewer.

Client management is the multiplier: a well-managed client relationship converts a project into a retainer, a referral, and a repeat customer. Poorly managed, the same project ends in disputes, unpaid invoices, and reputation damage that follows the independent for years.

```mermaid

    SOW["SOW: commitments defined"] --> DELIVER["Delivery: work and communication"]

    CHANGE --> HANDOVER["Handover: clean transition"]
    HANDOVER --> REFERRAL["Closure: reference and referral"]
```

Every phase is designed — the referral is earned at closure, not hoped for.

## Practical Applications

### Delivery Checklist

- [ ] Every engagement has a written SOW with acceptance criteria
- [ ] Communication cadence is agreed at kickoff and honored
- [ ] Change requests are documented and priced before work continues
- [ ] Deliverables pass the independent's own quality bar before handover
- [ ] Closure includes handover documentation and a written reference request

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Why It Is a Problem | Better Approach |
|---------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **Verbal commitments** | Memory disputes; scope invented retroactively | Written SOWs; email confirmations of decisions |
| **Silent delivery** | Clients anxious; trust erodes despite good work | Regular updates even when there is little news |
| **Free change absorption** | Margin evaporates; precedent set | Commercial change process, politely enforced |
| **Heroic overcommitment** | Multiple clients, missed deadlines, burned bridges | Capacity limits; honest scheduling |

## Success Indicators

- Projects close on time, on scope, with satisfied clients
- Most revenue comes from repeat clients and referrals
- Change requests are handled without conflict or margin loss
- Clients describe communication as a strength in testimonials
