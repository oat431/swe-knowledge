# Vision and Goals

> Product vision defines the future you're creating. Strategic goals define how you'll know you're getting there. Together, they provide direction and criteria for all product decisions.

## Why Vision and Goals Matter

**Without vision:**
- Features without direction
- Reactive decision-making
- Team confusion about priorities
- Difficulty saying no

**Without goals:**
- No way to measure progress
- Vision remains abstract
- Can't demonstrate success
- No accountability

**With vision and goals:**
- Clear direction for decisions
- Aligned team understanding
- Measurable progress
- Intentional product evolution

## Product Vision

### What Makes a Good Vision

**Characteristics:**
- **Inspiring:** Motivates and energizes
- **Clear:** Easy to understand and remember
- **Ambitious:** Stretches beyond current state
- **Achievable:** Possible with effort and time
- **Stable:** Doesn't change with every release

### Vision Formula

```
[In a world where...] [our target users...] [will be able to...] 
[which will result in...]
```

**Examples:**

```
Example 1 (Customer Service Platform):
In a world where customer expectations are rising, customer 
service representatives will be able to deliver personalized, 
efficient support using a unified view of customer information, 
which will result in satisfying experiences for customers and 
fulfilling work for representatives.

Example 2 (Project Management Tool):
In a world where teams are distributed and complex, project 
teams will be able to collaborate seamlessly and deliver 
projects on time without administrative overhead, which will 
result in successful projects and happy teams.

Example 3 (E-commerce Platform):
In a world where shoppers have infinite choices, small business 
owners will be able to create compelling online stores that 
compete with major retailers, which will result in thriving 
small businesses and diverse shopping options.
```

### Vision Testing

**Ask:**
- Can team members repeat it?
- Does it inspire and motivate?
- Does it guide decisions?
- Is it memorable?
- Does it connect to user value?

## Strategic Goals

### Goal Hierarchy

```
Product Vision (5+ years)
    ↓
Strategic Goals (1-3 years)
    ↓
Product Objectives (6-12 months)
    ↓
Initiatives and Features (current quarter)
```

### Types of Goals

**1. Business Goals**
- Revenue targets
- Market share
- Profitability
- Customer acquisition cost
- Lifetime value

**2. User Goals**
- User satisfaction (NPS, CSAT)
- Adoption rates
- Retention rates
- Task completion rates
- Time to value

**3. Product Goals**
- Performance metrics
- Quality metrics
- Engagement metrics
- Feature adoption
- Platform health

**4. Team Goals**
- Velocity and throughput
- Quality and reliability
- Team satisfaction
- Learning and growth

### SMART Goals

**Specific:** Clear and unambiguous
**Measurable:** Quantifiable criteria
**Achievable:** Realistic given resources
**Relevant:** Aligned with vision
**Time-bound:** Has a deadline

**Example:**
```
Weak goal: "Improve customer satisfaction"
SMART goal: "Increase customer satisfaction score from 3.2 to 
4.5 out of 5 by end of Q4 2026, measured by post-call surveys"
```

### Goal Setting Process

**1. Review vision**
- What future are we creating?

**2. Assess current state**
- Where are we now?
- What are current metrics?

**3. Identify gaps**
- What needs to change?
- How far do we need to go?

**4. Set ambitious but achievable goals**
- Stretch but don't break
- Balance ambition with realism

**5. Define success metrics**
- How will we measure?
- What data sources?

**6. Set timeline**
- When will we achieve this?
- What are intermediate milestones?

## Connecting Vision to Execution

### Strategy Cascade

```
Vision: Every customer service interaction is personalized, 
efficient, and satisfying.

Strategic Goal 1: Reduce average call handling time by 30% by Q4 2026
  → Objective: Provide unified customer view by Q2 2026
    → Initiative: Integrate CRM and order management systems
      → Feature: Customer profile page with order history

Strategic Goal 2: Increase customer satisfaction from 3.2 to 4.5 by Q4 2026
  → Objective: Reduce customer hold time by 50% by Q3 2026
    → Initiative: Implement real-time information access
      → Feature: Instant search across all customer data

Strategic Goal 3: Reduce representative turnover by 50% by Q4 2026
  → Objective: Improve rep satisfaction from 2.8 to 4.0 by Q3 2026
    → Initiative: Reduce manual data entry by 70%
      → Feature: Auto-populate customer information
```

### Decision Framework

**For any feature or initiative, ask:**
1. Does it align with our vision?
2. Which strategic goal does it support?
3. How will we measure its impact?
4. Is it the best way to achieve the goal?

**If you can't answer these, reconsider.**

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Vague Vision

**Wrong:** "Be the best customer service platform"
**Right:** "Every customer service interaction is personalized, efficient, and satisfying"

### 2. Too Many Goals

**Wrong:** 15 strategic goals
**Right:** 3-5 strategic goals

### 3. Output Goals

**Wrong:** "Ship 10 features by Q3"
**Right:** "Reduce call handling time by 30% by Q3"

### 4. Disconnected Goals

**Wrong:** Goals that don't connect to vision
**Right:** Goals that clearly advance vision

### 5. Unmeasurable Goals

**Wrong:** "Improve user experience"
**Right:** "Increase task completion rate from 60% to 85%"

## Communicating Vision and Goals

### Vision Communication

**Methods:**
- All-hands presentations
- Team meetings
- Product documentation
- Onboarding materials
- Office displays

**Frequency:**
- Repeat often
- Connect decisions to vision
- Celebrate progress toward vision

### Goal Communication

**Methods:**
- OKR documents
- Dashboards and reports
- Regular reviews
- Team planning sessions

**Frequency:**
- Quarterly planning
- Monthly reviews
- Weekly check-ins

## Senior-Level Vision and Goals

1. **Shape company vision**
   - Not just product vision
   - Influence business strategy
   - Connect product to company direction

2. **Set ambitious goals**
   - Stretch the organization
   - Balance ambition with realism
   - Inspire exceptional performance

3. **Align organization**
   - Connect teams to vision
   - Ensure goal alignment across functions
   - Build shared understanding

4. **Adapt over time**
   - Evolve vision as market changes
   - Adjust goals based on learning
   - Maintain strategic coherence

## Metrics

- Vision recall (can team repeat it?)
- Goal alignment (do initiatives connect to goals?)
- Goal achievement rate
- Time from goal setting to achievement
- Team motivation and clarity (survey)

## Checklist

Before finalizing vision and goals:
- [ ] Vision is clear, inspiring, and memorable
- [ ] Vision tested with team and stakeholders
- [ ] 3-5 strategic goals defined
- [ ] Goals are SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- [ ] Goals connect clearly to vision
- [ ] Success metrics defined for each goal
- [ ] Timeline and milestones set
- [ ] Goals prioritized
- [ ] Vision and goals communicated to team
- [ ] Vision and goals documented and accessible
