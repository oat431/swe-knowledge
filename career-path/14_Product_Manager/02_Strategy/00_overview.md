# Strategy

> Product strategy defines how a product will achieve its vision and create value. It connects customer needs, business goals, and market opportunities to specific product choices and priorities.

## Why Strategy Matters

Products without strategy:
- Ship features without direction
- React to requests instead of leading
- Lack coherence across releases
- Fail to create sustainable value

Products with strategy:
- Make intentional choices
- Say no to good ideas that don't fit
- Build coherent experiences over time
- Create defensible market positions

## Strategy Framework

### 1. Vision

**What future are we creating?**

Example:
```
Vision: Every customer service interaction is personalized, 
efficient, and satisfying for both the customer and the representative.
```

### 2. Strategic Goals

**What outcomes will we achieve?**

Example:
```
Goals:
- Reduce average call handling time by 30%
- Increase customer satisfaction from 3.2 to 4.5
- Reduce representative turnover by 50%
```

### 3. Target Users

**Who are we serving?**

Example:
```
Primary: Customer service representatives at mid-market companies
Secondary: Customer service managers
Tertiary: End customers calling support
```

### 4. Value Proposition

**Why will they choose us?**

Example:
```
For customer service representatives who need to handle calls 
efficiently, our unified customer platform provides instant access 
to all customer information in one view, unlike current solutions 
that require switching between multiple systems.
```

### 5. Strategic Choices

**What will we do and not do?**

Example:
```
We will:
- Focus on mid-market companies (100-1000 employees)
- Integrate with existing CRM systems
- Prioritize speed and simplicity

We will not:
- Target enterprise customers
- Build our own CRM
- Add complex customization features
```

## Strategy Development Process

### 1. Assess Current State

**Where are we now?**
- Current product performance
- Market position
- User satisfaction
- Business metrics
- Competitive landscape

### 2. Define Desired Future

**Where do we want to be?**
- Vision and goals
- Target market position
- User experience aspirations
- Business outcomes

### 3. Identify Opportunities

**What paths could get us there?**
- Market opportunities
- User needs
- Technology trends
- Competitive gaps

### 4. Make Choices

**Which path will we take?**
- Target users and segments
- Value proposition
- Key differentiators
- Strategic trade-offs

### 5. Plan Execution

**How will we get there?**
- Strategic initiatives
- Resource allocation
- Timeline and milestones
- Success metrics

## Senior-Level Strategy

1. **Shape product direction**
   - Not just execute roadmap
   - Influence company strategy
   - Identify new opportunities

2. **Make strategic trade-offs**
   - Say no to good ideas
   - Balance short-term and long-term
   - Manage stakeholder expectations

3. **Build strategic alignment**
   - Connect product to business
   - Align teams around strategy
   - Communicate strategic choices

4. **Adapt strategy over time**
   - Monitor market changes
   - Learn from outcomes
   - Evolve strategy intentionally
