# Roadmapping

> A roadmap communicates product direction, sequencing work to achieve strategic goals. It balances commitment with flexibility, showing what you'll build and when without false precision.

## Why Roadmapping Matters

Products without roadmaps:
- Teams don't know what's next
- Stakeholders can't plan
- No visible progress toward goals
- Constant prioritization debates

Products with roadmaps:
- Clear direction and sequence
- Stakeholders can coordinate
- Progress visible and trackable
- Priorities documented and stable

## Roadmapping Principles

1. **Outcome-focused:** Connect to goals, not just features
2. **Honest about uncertainty:** Use ranges, not false precision
3. **Audience-adapted:** Different detail for different stakeholders
4. **Living document:** Regular updates as you learn
5. **Strategic alignment:** Every item connects to strategy

## Roadmap Components

### Time Horizons

**Now (current quarter):**
- Committed work
- High confidence
- Detailed scope
- In execution

**Next (next quarter):**
- Planned work
- Medium confidence
- Scoped but flexible
- Ready to start

**Later (future quarters):**
- Potential work
- Low confidence
- High-level themes
- Subject to change

### Content Levels

**Themes:**
- Strategic focus areas
- Group related initiatives
- Connect to goals
- Example: "Customer efficiency"

**Initiatives:**
- Major pieces of work
- Multi-quarter scope
- Deliver theme outcomes
- Example: "Unified customer platform"

**Features:**
- Specific capabilities
- Quarter scope
- Deliver initiative value
- Example: "Customer profile page"

## Senior-Level Roadmapping

1. **Strategic roadmapping**
   - Not just feature lists
   - Connect to business strategy
   - Show market moves

2. **Manage uncertainty**
   - Communicate confidence levels
   - Plan for adaptation
   - Build in flexibility

3. **Align organization**
   - Connect team roadmaps
   - Coordinate dependencies
   - Build shared understanding

4. **Drive execution**
   - Translate to delivery plans
   - Track progress
   - Ensure outcomes achieved
