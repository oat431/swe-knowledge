# Prioritization

> Prioritization is the discipline of deciding what to do first, what to do later, and what not to do at all. It ensures limited resources create maximum value.

## Why Prioritization Matters

Products without prioritization:
- Build everything requested
- Spread resources too thin
- Ship low-value features
- Fail to achieve strategic goals

Products with prioritization:
- Focus on highest-value work
- Make intentional trade-offs
- Achieve strategic outcomes
- Say no with confidence

## Prioritization Principles

1. **Value-driven:** Prioritize by value, not volume
2. **Evidence-based:** Use data, not opinions
3. **Strategic alignment:** Connect to goals
4. **Transparent:** Make criteria and decisions visible
5. **Adaptive:** Re-prioritize as you learn

## Senior-Level Prioritization

1. **Make strategic trade-offs**
   - Not just tactical prioritization
   - Balance short-term and long-term
   - Manage stakeholder expectations

2. **Build prioritization capability**
   - Train teams in frameworks
   - Create prioritization processes
   - Establish decision governance

3. **Drive outcomes**
   - Connect priorities to results
   - Measure prioritization effectiveness
   - Learn and improve
