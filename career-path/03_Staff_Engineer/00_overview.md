# Staff Engineer

> **Positioning:** A senior individual-contributor path focused on technical influence across multiple teams, systems, or a major business area.

## What This Path Is

A staff engineer solves problems that are too broad, ambiguous, or cross-cutting for one team to resolve alone. The role is not simply a more experienced senior engineer. Its defining feature is the scale of influence: improving technical direction, alignment, and execution across team boundaries.

Staff engineers often work through proposals, architecture reviews, technical strategy, mentoring, and coordination rather than owning every implementation detail themselves.

## Primary Outcomes

- Coherent technical direction across multiple teams
- Reduced duplication and conflicting local decisions
- Major risks identified and addressed early
- Shared patterns, platforms, or practices that improve delivery
- Better decisions in areas where authority is distributed
- Increased technical capability across the organization

## Signals for Moving Forward

- Several teams depend on your ability to clarify a technical direction.
- You can create alignment among people with different incentives and priorities.
- Your proposals improve outcomes even when you do not implement the entire solution.
- You can distinguish a local optimization from a system-level improvement.
- You are comfortable operating with incomplete information and distributed authority.

## Evidence to Build

- Cross-team architecture proposal
- Technical strategy or platform roadmap
- Migration plan involving multiple teams
- Technology standards or reference architecture
- Trade-study report with stakeholder decisions
- Post-incident analysis that leads to organization-wide improvement
- Mentoring or community-of-practice plan
