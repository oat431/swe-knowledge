# Measurement

> **Core Principle:** A specialist quality engineer uses defect, coverage, flow, and quality-cost measures responsibly to drive improvement without creating perverse incentives.

## What Measurement Means at the Specialist Level

Measurement is:
- **Purposeful:** Every metric has a clear purpose and audience
- **Balanced:** Multiple metrics to avoid gaming and tunnel vision
- **Contextual:** Interpreted with context, not in isolation
- **Actionable:** Leads to decisions and improvements
- **Ethical:** Does not punish individuals or create perverse incentives

A specialist quality engineer knows **what** to measure, **how** to measure it, and **what not** to measure.

## Why This Capability Matters

Without proper measurement:
- Cannot identify quality problems
- Cannot justify quality investments
- Cannot track improvement
- Decisions based on gut feeling
- Quality initiatives fail

With proper measurement:
- Early problem detection
- Data-driven decisions
- Justified quality investments
- Tracked improvement over time
- Continuous quality improvement
