# Quality Engineering

> **Core Principle:** A specialist quality engineer prevents defects through engineering practices, not just finds them through testing.

## What Quality Engineering Means at the Specialist Level

Quality engineering is:
- **Prevention-focused:** Stop defects before they are created
- **Process-oriented:** Build quality into the development process
- **Systematic:** Use reviews, static analysis, and metrics
- **Cultural:** Foster a quality mindset across the team

A specialist quality engineer moves beyond "testing at the end" to **building quality in** from the start.

## Why This Capability Matters

Testing alone is not enough:
- Testing finds defects but does not prevent them
- Late testing is expensive to fix
- Some defects escape to production
- Quality is everyone's responsibility

Quality engineering delivers:
- Fewer defects introduced
- Lower cost of quality
- Faster delivery (less rework)
- Higher team morale
- Better customer satisfaction

You demonstrate mastery of quality engineering when you can:
- Implement defect prevention practices
- Conduct effective code reviews
- Configure and use static analysis tools
- Drive continuous improvement initiatives
- Define and use quality metrics
- Foster a quality culture in the team
